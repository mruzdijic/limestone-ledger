Printable postcard (6 × 4 in, front and back).

The QR code isn't set up yet. Open postcard.html, find SITE_URL near the bottom, and paste your GitHub Pages address.

To print: press Ctrl+P (Windows) or ⌘+P (Mac). Choose paper size 4×6 in (or "Index card"), set margins to None, scale 100%, and turn on Background graphics. For double-sided card stock, flip on the short edge. Or print on letter paper and trim. Always scan the printed QR code with a phone before handing cards out.

Kingston, 1844: the capital is leaving for Montreal, and a young clerk leaves behind four clues.

Scan the code, follow the clues on foot to four downtown landmarks, take a photo at each, and keep the scrapbook you make. About 30 to 60 minutes, all within a short walk.

A Queen's University student course prototype (ENIN 301, Team 37). Voluntary, adults 18+. Your photos stay on your phone and nothing personal is collected. Feedback at the end is anonymous and optional.
